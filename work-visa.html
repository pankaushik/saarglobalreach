Skilled Worker Visa

For professionals with job offers in skilled occupations. Available for UK, Canada, Australia, and other countries with high demand for skilled workforce.

Available in: UK, Canada, Australia, NZ
Duration: 2-5 Years
Key Requirements:

  • Job offer from approved sponsor
  • Salary threshold criteria
  • English language proficiency

H1B Visa (USA)

Specialty occupation visa for professionals working in the United States. One of the most competitive and rewarding work visas globally.

Destination: United States
Duration: 3 Years (Renewable)
Key Requirements:
  • Bachelor's degree or higher
  • Job offer in specialty occupation
  • Employer petition (I-129)
  • Annual quota limitations
Subject to annual lottery. Advanced degree holders get preference.

General Work Visa Requirements

  • Valid passport with minimum 6 months validity
  • Job offer letter from approved employer
  • Employment contract with salary details
  • Educational certificates and transcripts
  • Professional qualifications and licenses
  • Work experience letters and references
  • English language test results (if required)
  • Medical examination certificates
  • Police clearance certificate
  • Proof of financial stability
  • Passport-sized photographs
  • Visa application fees

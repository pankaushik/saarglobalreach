Foundation & Early Beginnings
Visa Point Consultants began its journey 30–35 years ago as a basic service provider, primarily specializing in passport applications. At a time when the Malwa region had very limited immigration consultancy services, students and families were forced to travel to distant metro cities like Chandigarh to seek reliable guidance. Recognizing this critical gap, Visa Point Consultants envisioned bringing trustworthy and comprehensive immigration services directly to Bathinda, laying the foundation for local access to global opportunities.

2
Establishment & Trust Building
Since 2005, Visa Point Consultants has been transforming aspirations into achievements by helping individuals realize their dreams of studying and settling abroad. By introducing structured immigration consultancy services in Bathinda, the organization made global education and migration guidance accessible at the local level. Through transparent processes, ethical practices, and honest guidance, the company earned strong public trust, which became the cornerstone of its long-term growth and reputation.
Since 2005
Idea Generation
3
Service Expansion & Global Integration
To further support aspiring students, Visa Point Consultants introduced IELTS and PTE coaching, strengthening its end-to-end service ecosystem. Over the years, the consultancy successfully guided thousands of students toward higher education abroad, helping them build brighter futures across leading international destinations. As competition in the immigration industry intensified, the company stood apart by pioneering direct tie-ups with colleges and universities. These partnerships enabled authentic information, transparent admissions, and direct institutional access. Additionally, direct overseas visits reinforced its commitment to accuracy, trust, and global exposure.
Since 2005
Idea Generation
4
Infrastructure Growth & International Expansion
The organization’s steady growth is reflected in its physical and geographical expansion. From a modest 600 sq. ft. office, Visa Point Consultants grew into a multi-storied, 12,000 sq. ft. Head Office in Bathinda — a symbol of dedication, progress, and credibility. Today, the consultancy has expanded its footprint beyond Punjab, with offices across North India, including Chandigarh and Haryana, and internationally in the UK and Dubai. Despite this growth, its core values remain unchanged: integrity, student-first guidance, and excellence in service. From humble beginnings to international recognition, the journey continues with one mission — to turn global dreams into reality, one success story at a time.

Leadership & Legacy

Visa Point Consultants was founded in 2005 by Mr. Jaswinder Singh Sidhu
Bahia in Bathinda as an immigration and student consultancy firm. In 2014,
leadership was assumed by the current Managing Director, Mr. Navpreet Sidhu
Bahia, who has significantly strengthened the organization through his industry
expertise, strategic vision, and commitment to excellence.
Under his leadership, Mr. Bahia has dedicated extensive efforts to visa counselling
and testing proficiency, achieving multiple high scores across PTE, IELTS,
Student, Spouse, and Visitor visa categories. His consistent results and high
levels of client satisfaction have earned him the professional recognition of “Visa
King.”

Global Engagement & Accreditation

The Visa Point Consultants team actively participates in international workshops
and institutional collaborations in countries such as Canada and the United
Kingdom, USA, Europe, Dubai, Muscat, Oman, and many more, fostering
strong relationships with global universities and industry partners. The firm holds
recognized authorizations from IDP Australia, Pearson Test of English, and the
British Council, and maintains direct affiliations with numerous universities,
ensuring accurate and up-to-date information for visa and admission processes.

Experience & Performance

With over 20 years of experience in the immigration sector, Visa Point Consultants places strong emphasis on educating clients about immigration regulations, ensuring compliance, accuracy, and transparency. Our services are designed to deliver efficient and timely visa outcomes, supported by an annual success rate of approximately 99%. Since its establishment, Visa Point Consultants has successfully processed over 1,000 visas annually, assisting clients seeking opportunities in destinations such as the United Kingdom, Canada, Australia, New Zealand, Europe, USA, Cyprus, Dubai and Singapore.

Continued Growth

In line with its expansion strategy, Visa Point Consultants has extended its services to Chandigarh, Haryana, in the north region of India; and even across national borders to UK and Dubai; reinforcing its commitment to making high- quality visa and immigration services accessible while continuing to support students in realizing their international education goals.

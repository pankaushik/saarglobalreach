Questions & Answer

Q1. Which is the prerequisites for immigration?

Every immigration journey starts with preparation. Typically, you will need a valid passport, educational and professional credentials, proof of funds, language proficiency like IELTS or PTE, and a clean background record.

Q3. What IELTS Score required for Canada?

For most Canadian immigration programs, typically an overall IELTS band of 6.0-7.0, with 6.0 or above in each skill, is required.

Q4. How much time needed for visa renewal?

Visa renewals take time, and it is best to plan ahead. Processing usually ranges from 4 to 12 weeks, depending on your visa type.

Q5. What are the prerequisites for immigration to the United Kingdom?

To immigrate to the UK, you will need a valid passport, proof of financial stability, English language proficiency, relevant qualifications, and a clean criminal record.

Q6. What IELTS score is required for Australia?

For most Australian visas, typically an overall IELTS band of 6.0-7.0, with at least 6.0 in each section is required.

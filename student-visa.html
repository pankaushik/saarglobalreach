Student Visa Requirements

  • Valid passport with at least 6 months validity
  • Offer letter / Letter of Acceptance from university
  • Proof of financial support (bank statements, loan approval)
  • Academic transcripts and certificates
  • English language proficiency (IELTS/PTE/TOEFL)
  • Statement of Purpose (SOP)
  • Health and medical certificates
  • Police clearance certificate
  • Passport-sized photographs
  • Visa application form and fees
